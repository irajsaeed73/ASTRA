 **List of tasks** for the **3-Month Goal Plan** to lay the foundation for HLC accreditation:


### **1. Conduct a Comprehensive Gap Analysis (2 Weeks)**
- **Tasks:**
  1. Research HLC's **five Criteria for Accreditation**.
  2. Evaluate current institutional practices against HLC requirements.
  3. Identify gaps in mission alignment, integrity, teaching and learning, resources, and institutional effectiveness.
  4. Prepare a detailed gap analysis report.
  5. Present findings to the accreditation task force for review.


### **2. Develop an Accreditation Task Force (1 Month)**
- **Tasks:**
  1. Identify and recruit key stakeholders (faculty, staff, administrators, and board members).
  2. Define roles and responsibilities for each task force member.
  3. Schedule the first task force meeting to outline the accreditation roadmap.
  4. Develop a communication plan for task force activities.


### **3. Submit Eligibility Application (2 Months and 2 Weeks)**
- **Tasks:**
  1. Gather all required documentation for the Eligibility Application.
  2. Draft the Eligibility Application, ensuring alignment with HLC standards.
  3. Review and finalize the application with the task force.
  4. Submit the Eligibility Application to HLC.
  5. Address any feedback or requests for additional information from HLC promptly.


### **4. Launch Compliance Training (3 Months)**
- **Tasks:**
  1. Develop a training curriculum focused on HLC's **five Criteria for Accreditation**.
  2. Create training materials (e.g., presentations, handouts, quizzes).
  3. Schedule training sessions for faculty and staff.
  4. Track participation and ensure 100% completion of training.
  5. Gather feedback from participants to improve future training sessions.


### **5. Engage Stakeholders (3 Months)**
- **Tasks:**
  1. Develop a stakeholder communication plan.
  2. Identify key stakeholders (students, alumni, external partners).
  3. Organize informational sessions or workshops to explain the accreditation process.
  4. Collect feedback from stakeholders to identify additional areas for improvement.
  5. Create a feedback loop to keep stakeholders informed about progress.


### **6. Monitor Progress and Adjust Plans (Ongoing)**
- **Tasks:**
  1. Hold weekly task force meetings to review progress.
  2. Track KPIs for each key action (e.g., gap analysis completion, training participation).
  3. Address any challenges or roadblocks promptly.
  4. Update the accreditation roadmap as needed based on feedback and progress.


### **Summary of Tasks:**
- **Weeks 1-2:** Conduct gap analysis and prepare report.
- **Weeks 3-4:** Form the accreditation task force and define roles.
- **Weeks 5-10:** Prepare and submit the Eligibility Application.
- **Weeks 11-12:** Launch compliance training and engage stakeholders.
- **Ongoing:** Monitor progress, hold meetings, and adjust plans as needed.

This task list ensures a structured and efficient approach to achieving the 3-month goal of laying the foundation for HLC accreditation.
