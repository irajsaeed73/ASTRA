### **Issue Title:**  
**3-Month Goal Plan: Laying the Foundation for HLC Accreditation**

### **Description:**  
This issue outlines a structured plan to lay the groundwork for achieving accreditation from the Higher Learning Commission (HLC). The plan focuses on conducting a comprehensive gap analysis, forming an accreditation task force, submitting the Eligibility Application, launching compliance training, engaging stakeholders, and monitoring progress. Each phase includes specific tasks with clear deliverables to ensure alignment with HLC standards.

### **Goals:**  
1. Understand HLC's **five Criteria for Accreditation** and evaluate institutional readiness.  
2. Identify and address gaps in institutional practices.  
3. Establish an effective accreditation task force with defined roles.  
4. Submit the HLC Eligibility Application successfully.  
5. Ensure compliance with HLC standards through targeted training.  
6. Engage key stakeholders and maintain transparent communication.  
7. Monitor progress and adapt the plan based on feedback.


### **Task Breakdown:**

#### **1. Conduct a Comprehensive Gap Analysis**  
- Research HLC's **five Criteria for Accreditation**.  
- Evaluate current institutional practices against HLC requirements.  
- Identify gaps in mission alignment, integrity, teaching and learning, resources, and institutional effectiveness.  
- Prepare a detailed gap analysis report.  
- Present findings to the accreditation task force for review.  

#### **2. Develop an Accreditation Task Force**  
- Identify and recruit key stakeholders (faculty, staff, administrators, and board members).  
- Define roles and responsibilities for each task force member.  
- Schedule the first task force meeting to outline the accreditation roadmap.  
- Develop a communication plan for task force activities.  

#### **3. Submit Eligibility Application**  
- Gather all required documentation for the Eligibility Application.  
- Draft the Eligibility Application, ensuring alignment with HLC standards.  
- Review and finalize the application with the task force.  
- Submit the Eligibility Application to HLC.  
- Address any feedback or requests for additional information from HLC promptly.  

#### **4. Launch Compliance Training**  
- Develop a training curriculum focused on HLC's **five Criteria for Accreditation**.  
- Create training materials (e.g., presentations, handouts, quizzes).  
- Schedule training sessions for faculty and staff.  
- Track participation and ensure 100% completion of training.  
- Gather feedback from participants to improve future training sessions.  

#### **5. Engage Stakeholders**  
- Develop a stakeholder communication plan.  
- Identify key stakeholders (students, alumni, external partners).  
- Organize informational sessions or workshops to explain the accreditation process.  
- Collect feedback from stakeholders to identify additional areas for improvement.  
- Create a feedback loop to keep stakeholders informed about progress.  

#### **6. Monitor Progress and Adjust Plans**  
- Hold regular task force meetings to review progress.  
- Track KPIs for each key action (e.g., gap analysis completion, training participation).  
- Address any challenges or roadblocks promptly.  
- Update the accreditation roadmap as needed based on feedback and progress.

---

### **Deliverables:**  
1. Comprehensive gap analysis report.  
2. Accreditation task force formed with roles defined.  
3. Eligibility Application submitted to HLC.  
4. Compliance training completed for all faculty and staff.  
5. Stakeholder engagement sessions conducted.  
6. Progress reports and updated roadmap.
 

### **Timeline:**  
3 months
