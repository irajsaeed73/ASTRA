### Issue Name:
Prepare and Submit Eligibility Application Aligned with HLC's 5 Criteria for Accreditation

### Description:
This task involves preparing and submitting the Eligibility Application to the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), ensuring alignment with HLC's five Criteria for Accreditation. The application must demonstrate the institution's compliance with these criteria and include all required documentation. The goal is to submit a comprehensive and accurate application that reflects the institution's commitment to meeting HLC standards.

### Alignment with HLC's 5 Criteria for Accreditation:
The Eligibility Application must address each of HLC's five Criteria for Accreditation. Below is a breakdown of how the application preparation will align with these criteria:

### Criterion 1: Mission
#### • Key Focus:
The institution’s mission is clear, articulated publicly, and guides its operations.
#### • Tasks:
- Include the institution’s mission statement and demonstrate how it is communicated to stakeholders.
- Provide evidence of how the mission guides decision-making, resource allocation, and institutional goals.
- Highlight alignment between the mission and academic programs, student services, and community engagement.

### Criterion 2: Integrity: Ethical and Responsible Conduct
#### • Key Focus:
The institution operates with integrity, ensuring ethical and responsible conduct in all practices.
#### • Tasks:
- Submit policies and procedures related to ethical conduct, governance, and transparency.
- Provide examples of how the institution ensures accountability (e.g., board oversight, conflict of interest policies).
- Demonstrate compliance with federal, state, and accrediting agency regulations.

### Criterion 3: Teaching and Learning: Quality, Resources, and Support
#### • Key Focus:
The institution provides high-quality teaching and learning experiences, supported by adequate resources.
#### • Tasks:
- Submit documentation on academic programs, faculty qualifications, and student learning outcomes.
- Provide evidence of resources dedicated to teaching and learning (e.g., library services, technology, faculty development).
- Highlight student support services (e.g., advising, tutoring, career services) and their impact on student success.

### Criterion 4: Teaching and Learning: Evaluation and Improvement
#### • Key Focus:
The institution demonstrates a commitment to evaluating and improving its teaching and learning practices.
#### • Tasks:
- Submit assessment plans and reports showing how student learning outcomes are measured and used for improvement.
- Provide examples of program reviews, curriculum updates, and faculty development initiatives.
- Highlight institutional effectiveness processes and how they inform decision-making.

### Criterion 5: Institutional Effectiveness, Resources, and Planning
#### • Key Focus:
The institution demonstrates effectiveness in achieving its mission, supported by sound planning and resource management.
#### • Tasks:
- Submit strategic plans, financial statements, and budget reports showing fiscal stability and resource allocation.
- Provide evidence of institutional effectiveness (e.g., retention rates, graduation rates, alumni success).
- Highlight planning processes and how they align with the institution’s mission and goals.

### Key Deliverables:
1. **Eligibility Application Draft:**
   - Draft the application, ensuring each section addresses the relevant HLC criterion.
   - Include narratives, evidence, and documentation for each criterion.

2. **Documentation Package:**
   - Compile all required documents, organized by criterion.
   - Ensure documents are accurate, complete, and professionally formatted.

3. **Submission:**
   - Submit the completed Eligibility Application to HLC within the 2-month and 2-week deadline.
   - Confirm receipt of the application by HLC.

4. **Feedback Response:**
   - Address any feedback or requests for additional information from HLC promptly and thoroughly.

### Acceptance Criteria:
- Eligibility Application is submitted to HLC within the specified timeline (2 months and 2 weeks).
- All sections of the application align with HLC's five Criteria for Accreditation.
- Required documentation is included and meets HLC standards.
- Feedback from HLC is addressed within 1 week of receipt.

### Dependencies:
- Input and review from the Accreditation Task Force.
- Collaboration with departments such as Academic Affairs, Finance, Student Services, and Institutional Research.
- Access to institutional data, policies, and procedures.

### Timeline:
- **Week 1-2:** Gather and organize required documentation, aligned with HLC's five criteria.
- **Week 3-4:** Draft the Eligibility Application, ensuring alignment with each criterion.
- **Week 5-6:** Review and finalize the application with the Accreditation Task Force.
- **Week 7-8:** Submit the application to HLC.
- **Week 9-10:** Address any feedback from HLC.



### Notes:
- Regular updates should be provided to the Accreditation Task Force and key stakeholders.
- Use a shared repository (e.g., Google Drive, SharePoint) to store and organize documents for easy access.
- Schedule regular check-ins to monitor progress and address challenges.

This task will be considered complete when the Eligibility Application is successfully submitted to HLC and any feedback is addressed.
